Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 14, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to pbsNet. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform for centralized identity management and employee tools.

Information We Collect

Google OAuth Data: When you log in with Google, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture URL. This is used solely to create and authenticate your account on pbsNet.

Profile Information: We store professional details such as your designation, PBS name, office name, and contact information that you voluntarily provide to maintain your digital identity hub.

Appwrite Logs: Our backend (Appwrite) may collect technical data such as IP addresses and device information for security and session management purposes.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the pbsNet Identity Hub.
  • To authenticate your identity across our connected automation tools (like Data3Fez).
  • To facilitate internal communication between PBS employees.
  • To ensure the security of our platform and prevent unauthorized access.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, powered by Appwrite Cloud, to protect your data. Your information is encrypted during transmission and stored securely. We do not sell or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your profile information at any time within the pbsNet application. For permanent account deletion or data portability requests, please contact our administrator.
